# Nyx — Product vision

Product definition · 31 October 2026

## Positioning

**Nyx is a personal AI companion that helps older adults live independently at home, on their own terms.**

**Your own space. Your own routine. A little help from Nyx.**

Nyx is for older adults living at home, especially those living alone who want help remembering everyday details and a natural companion to talk to. The person themselves or their family may buy and set up Nyx; the older adult remains the primary user.

## The product

Nyx is a small, stationary device with a camera, microphone, and speaker. Place it in a room and say “Hey Nyx.” Ask naturally, get a simple spoken answer, and continue the conversation.

The promise is **“Help me remember what happened around me.”** Find belongings, recall useful moments, and spend less time searching or asking someone else for help.

One device can only answer from the room and moments it captured. Supporting recordings are available when wanted; conversation remains the main experience.

## Features

Nyx helps you **find things, remember people and moments, get answers, and follow through on plans.**

| Feature | What you can ask |
| --- | --- |
| Find your belongings | “Where did I keep my glasses?” |
| Remember people and visits | “Who came by today?” Recognize familiar people with their consent and enrollment, within camera coverage. |
| Recall what happened | “What happened in this room this morning?” |
| Remember what you said | “Where did I say I would keep my phone?” or “Was there anything else I asked you to remember?” |
| Answer everyday questions | “Can you explain how this works?” |
| Keep reminders | “Remind me to call my daughter at six.” |
| Reach trusted people | “Call my daughter.” |
| Be there for a conversation | “Hey Nyx, what's up?” |

## Security and privacy

**Your recordings and personal memory stay on your device.** Nyx uses local vision models, local language models, and local speech recognition and generation for core recall and conversation. Private images, audio, and conversations stay on the device for that processing.

- **Stored on your device:** recordings and personal history stay under your control, with no automatic cloud upload.
- **Processed on your device:** seeing, understanding, listening, and speaking run locally.
- **Connected only when needed:** calls and online searches use external services, with only the information needed for the requested action.
- **Control and protection:** pause recording, mute the microphone, and delete history. Encrypted storage, restricted access, and secure updates protect personal information. You choose who can access your history, including family members.

Local processing reduces exposure to cloud services; it works alongside device security and access controls.

## Personality and trust

Calm, relaxed, attentive, and a little witty—a perceptive friend with a dry sense of humor who does not need to fill every silence. **Easy to talk to, not clever to listen to.** Use short sentences and familiar spoken words. Never patronize someone or joke about forgetting things.

Keep what Nyx saw distinct from what someone told it. Say “You told me you were going to keep it in the kitchen” when that is the evidence. Be honest about uncertainty and missing coverage.

Independence includes control: clear recording indicators, simple pause and mute controls, and intentional access to personal history. Family involvement respects the person's choices.

## Pitch

> Nyx helps older adults live independently at home. A small companion device that can see, hear, and talk, Nyx helps you find your belongings, remember people and everyday moments, manage reminders, and reach people you trust—just by asking. It is a personal concierge and a natural companion, with your recordings and core AI processing kept on your device. Your own space. Your own routine. A little help from Nyx.
